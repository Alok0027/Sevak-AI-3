# SevakAI logo

Three original directions. Open `sheet.html` in a browser to compare them
at the sizes and conditions that actually decide a mark: a 16px browser
tab, one colour on a photocopied form, and reversed on the brand green.
`small.html` compares the full mark against the small-size variant.

## Why these are original

The Ashoka Lion Capital is restricted by the State Emblem of India
(Prohibition of Improper Use) Act, 2005, and the NHM and Ayushman Bharat
marks belong to their programmes. None of them is used or imitated here.
What these borrow is the *genre*: the roundel format, the bilingual
lockup, and single-ink reproducibility.

## Files

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `a-record.svg` / `-mono` / `-reversed` | Direction A, Rail & Record |
| `b-shirorekha.svg` / `-mono` / `-reversed` | Direction B, Shirorekha |
| `b-shirorekha-small.svg` | Direction B below 24px (favicon, app icon) |
| `c-threshold.svg` / `-mono` / `-reversed` | Direction C, Threshold |

`-mono` is one ink for stamps, forms and fax. `-reversed` is for placing
on the forest green. Each is an authored file, not a CSS filter, so what
you see is what a printer gets.

## Colours

Straight from the product's design system — `mobile/lib/theme/tokens.dart`
and `dashboard/src/styles/tokens.css`. Forest `#14624A`, Ink `#10221C`,
Paper `#F7F9F8`. Changing a brand colour means changing all three.

## Lockup

Mark, then `SevakAI` set in Anek SemiBold with `-0.02em` tracking, with
`सेवक · आशा सहायक` in Hind Medium beneath it. Clear space around the mark
is half its height on every side.
