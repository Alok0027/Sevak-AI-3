## Schedule of home visits after delivery

"6 visits in case of institutional deliveries (Days 3, 7, 14, 21, 28 & 42)".

"7 visits in case of home deliveries (Days 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 28 & 42)" --
the extra day-1 visit exists because a baby born at home has had no
facility check at all.

## Low birth weight and what to do about it

Below 2.5 kg: advise the mother to keep the baby extra warm and to feed
more frequently.

Below 1.8 kg: refer to the nearest Sick Newborn Care Unit (SNCU).

A baby not gaining weight on a subsequent visit is referred to the SNCU
regardless of the birth weight -- the trend matters as much as the number.

## Newborn danger signs requiring referral

Refer a newborn with any of:

- no stool within 24 hours, or no urine within 48 hours of birth
- jaundice within the first 24 hours, or persisting after 14 days
- bleeding from any site, including the umbilicus
- poor feeding, excessive sleepiness or lethargy
- excessive weight loss despite breastfeeding
- rapid breathing, more than 60 breaths a minute, or chest retractions
- abnormal jerky movements or convulsions
- extremes of body temperature, or cold to touch
- bluish discolouration of the tongue and lips
- abdominal distension with repeated vomiting
- umbilical discharge, swelling or redness
- pallor, more than 10 skin pustules, or large boils
- redness of the eye with discharge of pus
