## Blood pressure thresholds in pregnancy

"If the BP is high (more than 140/90 mmHg; or diastolic more than 90
mmHg), check the BP again after 1 hour." A single elevated reading is not
by itself a diagnosis; a reading that remains high after an hour is.

"If the diastolic BP of the woman is above 110 mmHg, it is a danger sign
pointing towards imminent eclampsia. Such a woman must be referred to the
community health centre (CHC)/FRU IMMEDIATELY."

Pre-eclampsia is diagnosed where high blood pressure and proteinuria are
present together; refer the woman.

## Danger signs in pregnancy requiring immediate referral

Conditions demanding immediate referral to a first referral unit:

- "Any bleeding P/V during pregnancy, and heavy (>500 ml) vaginal bleeding
  during and following delivery"
- "Severe headache with blurred vision"
- "Convulsions or loss of consciousness"
- "Labour lasting longer than 12 hours"
- "Failure of the placenta to come out within 30 minutes of delivery"
- "Preterm labour (labour starting before 9 gestational months)"
- "Premature or prelabour rupture of membranes (PROM)"
- "Continuous severe abdominal pain"

Severe headache with blurred vision, and convulsions, sit in this list
because they are the presenting signs of imminent eclampsia -- they are
urgent whatever the blood pressure reading happens to be.

## Signs requiring a visit to the PHC within 24 hours

- "High fever with or without abdominal pain, and feels too weak to get out
  of bed"
- "Fast or difficult breathing"
- "Decreased or absent foetal movements"

## Schedule of antenatal visits

"Every pregnant woman makes at least 4 visits for ANC, including the first
visit/registration."

First visit at registration, ideally before 12 weeks. Second visit between
the 6th and 7th month, around 28 weeks. Third visit in the 7th month, at 32
weeks. Fourth visit in the 9th month, at 36 weeks.

## Iron and folic acid supplementation in pregnancy

Prophylactic: "All pregnant women need to be given one tablet of IFA (100
mg elemental iron and 0.5 mg folic acid) every day for at least 100 days,
starting after the first trimester at 16-18 weeks of gestation."

Therapeutic, for haemoglobin below 11 g/dL: two IFA tablets per day for
three months, requiring at least 200 tablets.

Haemoglobin below 7 g/dL, or breathlessness and tachycardia attributable to
anaemia: therapeutic IFA and referral to the medical officer.
